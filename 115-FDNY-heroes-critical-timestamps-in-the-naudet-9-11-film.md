## ON AMERICAN HISTORY
# FDNY Heroes: Critical Timestamps in the Naudet 9/11 Film
## A Raw Copy of the Documentary Was Released on YouTube
#911attacks, #fdny, #newyork, #history, #terrorism

![A YouTube user noted, "Something profound is at [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZHPzeXXwkZM&t=240s), he points the camera up at the Twin Towers, in their last moments of normalcy."](images/115-01.jpeg)

**THE TWENTY-FIFTH ANNIVERSARY** of the 9/11 Attacks recently passed. On that fateful day, the Naudet film (pronounced Naw-Doh) captured the event's blunt rawness. The recording won many awards, including a Peabody and an Emmy. Some argue the artifact is one of the most critical films in American history, alongside difficult watches like the [Zapruder film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zapruder_film).

Two French brothers captured the cataclysmic event. It began with Gédéon and Jules Naudet documenting an FDNY crew probie, Antonios "Tony" Benetatos. After weeks of inaction, Jules was "practicing filming" as firefighters investigated a gas leak. In a twist of fate, Jules captured a plane entering the World Trade Center. With permission, he trailed FDNY Chief [Joseph W. Pfeifer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_W._Pfeifer), who set up the fire command station in the North Tower.

Soon after, Mr. Naudet captured the faces of firefighters and first responders who would perish in heroic acts. By luck and timing, Jules and Chief Pfeifer survived both tower collapses. Following, the original tape was turned into a curated documentary, with the uncut film remaining under copyright protection. *Fair enough.* CNN later acquired the material in 2016.

However, on the twenty-fifth anniversary, copies of Naudet's raw film, which most of the public has not seen, [were released](https://www.reddit.com/r/911archive/comments/1wdmg3c/the_full_uncut_naudet_brothers_footage_is_now/) on YouTube without CNN's permission. In this author's view, the source film should remain up out of respect for its historical impact-testing the ultimate fair-use challenge. And copyright protections may eventually take everything down.

Within hours, YouTubers captured many important timestamps from key figures. For instance, YouTuber [pre911wtc1](https://www.youtube.com/@pre911wtc1) [placed a clock](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZHPzeXXwkZM) on the release, making the viewing experience profoundly impactful. These moving timestamps are burned into the American subconscious, and the edit lets the viewer see the unflinching reality that became Ground Zero.

What follows are harrowing quotes from the film, along with identifications. Special thanks to @[BrettClement-89](https://www.youtube.com/@BrettClement-89) for helping with this work. This short visual essay captures the best frames of heroes leading rescue efforts, along with poignant summaries.

Quotes with page numbers are from *Ordinary Heroes* by FDNY Chief Joseph Pfeifer. Caption timecodes use elapsed video runtime; the burned-in clock shows a third-party estimate of local wall-clock time.

When Jules and Chief Pfeifer returned to the firehouse later, "a man and woman had appeared . . . and introduced themselves as FBI agents." They took the tape and returned it weeks later.

Until September 2026, many never watched the film end to end because the media wasn't readily available. Now, the raw Naudet film gives the public a perspective-for young or old-to watch and reflect on before it's taken off the Internet.
